How this calculator works
This uses the Chinese Official / Competition Mahjong Rules (MCR) scoring system that playmahjong.io runs on. A winning hand needs at least 8 fan (before flowers) to be valid. Tap every scoring pattern the winning hand actually contains from the categorised list below — this calculator doesn't read tiles for you, so it trusts you to apply the Non‑Repeat (don't double count an implied pattern), Non‑Identical (one set can't score the same rule twice) and Exclusionary rules yourself, same as a real scorer would.

Self‑drawn win: every other player pays the winner (hand value + 8).
Discard win: the player who fed the winning tile pays (hand value + 8), the other two just pay 8 each.

Scores are saved on this device only (via your browser's local storage) — they won't sync between your phone and PC automatically.
